🛡️ How to spot phishing
  • Check the sender's email address carefully — look for typos or lookalike domains
  • Hover over links before clicking to preview the real URL
  • Urgent or threatening language ("Act now!", "Your account will be suspended") is a red flag
  • Legitimate services never ask for your password via email
  • When in doubt, go directly to the website — never click the link
  • Unexpected attachments — even from known senders — can contain malware

🚨 What to do if you clicked a real phishing link
  • Disconnect from the internet immediately
  • Change your password for the targeted service from a different device
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) if not already on
  • Report it to your IT/security team right away
  • Run a malware scan on your device
